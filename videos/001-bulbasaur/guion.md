### 3.4 · El nombre
- **Visual:** `GRAFICO`: "Fushigidane" escrito en crayón que se separa en "fushigi" + "tane"; después "Bulb" + "dinosaur".

**EN:** In Japanese, it's called Fushigidane. "Fushigi" means mysterious or strange, and "tane" means seed: the mysterious seed. It's also a pun on "fushigi da ne", which roughly means "isn't that strange?" In English, it's simple: bulb plus dinosaur. Bulbasaur.
